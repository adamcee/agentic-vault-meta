# Idea: Explain on the Homepage Which AI Habits the Protocols Fix

## Proposal
Add a homepage section that maps protocols to the AI habits they counter:
- **Slop.** The writing discipline and ingestion gate strip filler words ("delve," "synergy") and throat-clearing.
- **Sycophancy.** Decision records and research traceability ground answers in recorded decisions and evidence, not in agreement with the last thing the user said.
- **Dependency.** The human owns `AGENTS.md` and approves decisions, so planning stays with the person and execution goes to the AI.
