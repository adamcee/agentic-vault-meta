# PDR-P003: Writing Discipline Adopts ISO 24495-1 Plain Language

## Context
The writing rules drew on Strunk & White, military correspondence, and engineering practice. None of these is an international standard, and none frames writing around what the reader needs to do.

## Decision
The writing discipline adopts the four governing principles of ISO 24495-1:2023 (*Plain language — Part 1: Governing principles and guidelines*): readers get what they need (relevant), find it easily (findable), understand it easily (understandable), and can use it (usable). Two rules follow from it: define terms on first use, and write headings that say what the section contains.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Cite the U.S. Federal Plain Language Guidelines instead.** Rejected. They are national and government-focused; the ISO standard applies to any writer in any language.

## Consequences
- The template style guide, the website writing-discipline page, and [[writing-discipline]] add the four principles and the two rules.
- `sources.md` lists the standard.
- The 10-point tightening checklist is unchanged.
